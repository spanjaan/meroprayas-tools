About

About MeroPrayas

MeroPrayas is a browser-based utility toolbox designed to provide useful tools directly in your browser. It is a single-page application that works online and offline, without accounts, uploads or queues.

Mission & purpose

MeroPrayas is built as a fast, private, offline-first toolkit for everyday image, text and PDF work. The idea is simple: whenever a task can be done in your browser, it should be — no waiting for uploads, no registration, no watermarks, no hidden limits.

Tools

Image Compressor

Compress, resize and edit images directly in the browser. Adjust quality, choose the output format, crop with ratio presets, rotate, flip, straighten with perspective correction, resize to a preset or custom size, and process multiple images at once.

Unicode Converter

Convert Nepali text between Unicode, Preeti and Hisab. Both conversion directions are available with live conversion, format swapping, copy and download. Conversion is pure text mapping performed locally in your browser.

PDF Tools

Merge PDFs with drag & drop ordering, split by ranges, selected pages or one page per file, compress with clear quality trade-offs, turn images into a PDF, or export PDF pages as JPG images.

Offline-first philosophy

MeroPrayas is designed with an offline-first approach. Wherever technically possible, processing happens locally in the user's browser rather than sending files to a remote server. After the first visit, the service worker caches the app shell so the tools keep working without a connection.

Privacy-focused by design

Files you select are used to run the tool you chose — they are not intentionally collected, stored or uploaded by the application. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. See the Privacy Policy for the full, accurate picture.

Guide

How It Works

Every tool follows the same simple pattern: choose a tool, add your files or text, adjust the options, let MeroPrayas process everything locally, preview and download.

The general workflow

  1. 01

    Choose a tool

    Pick Image Compressor, Unicode Converter or the PDF tools from the dashboard.

  2. 02

    Select your file or enter text

    Drag & drop or browse for files, or paste the text you want to convert.

  3. 03

    Adjust the options

    Set quality, format, crop, resize, page size, split ranges and similar options.

  4. 04

    Process locally

    Processing runs in your browser. Your files are not intentionally uploaded.

  5. 05

    Preview the result

    Review sizes, dimensions and output before you commit or download.

  6. 06

    Download

    Save the finished file to your device, or download everything at once.

Tool-by-tool guide

Image Compressor

Compress, resize and edit your images in the browser.

  1. Select images — drag & drop or choose files (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG). You can add many at once.
  2. Edit (optional) — each image opens in the editor where you can crop, rotate, flip, straighten and resize.
  3. Adjust compression — set the quality slider and pick the output format (original, WebP, JPEG or PNG).
  4. Preview — check the expected result with the live preview before compressing.
  5. Compress & download — run compression on one image or all, then download the result.

Unicode Converter

Convert Nepali text between Unicode, Preeti and Hisab.

  1. Enter or paste text — type your text, paste it, or use the Paste button.
  2. Choose the direction — select From / To (Unicode, Preeti or Hisab) or use the swap button.
  3. Convert — live conversion updates as you type, or press Convert for a manual pass.
  4. Copy or download — copy the converted text or download it as a UTF-8 .txt file.

PDF Tools

Merge, split, compress and convert PDF files.

  1. Choose an operation — Merge, Split, Compress, JPG to PDF or PDF to JPG from the PDF toolbar.
  2. Select files — drag & drop or browse for the PDFs or images you need.
  3. Adjust the options — reorder files, set split ranges, pick a compression level, page size or output DPI.
  4. Process locally — PDFs and images are processed in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  5. Preview & download — check summaries and download individual files or a ZIP of everything.

Overview

MeroPrayas provides browser-based utility tools and is designed to process user-selected content locally where supported. This policy explains what the application does with the information and files you use while it runs.

Information we do not intentionally collect

The application does not intentionally collect, store or upload your files to a remote server when the corresponding tool operates entirely locally. Selecting a file does not automatically upload it anywhere. MeroPrayas has no user accounts and no built-in upload endpoints in the shipped build.

Local file processing

Files you select may be read and processed in your browser using local browser APIs such as File, Canvas, Blob and object URLs. Generated results live in browser memory and are revealed only through the app's download buttons. Please be aware that browsers, operating systems, browser extensions and other software on your device have their own privacy behavior that is outside this application's control.

Browser storage

MeroPrayas uses a small amount of browser storage on your device:

  • localStorage — stores two small settings values: your chosen theme (pixelpress-theme) and your last compression preferences such as quality, format and resize size (pixelpress-settings).
  • Cache Storage (service worker) — the service worker precaches the app shell (HTML, CSS, scripts, local fonts and icons) so the app opens offline after your first visit, and runtime-caches same-origin requests it needs.

The application does not use IndexedDB or sessionStorage. Everything stored is kept in your browser on your own device and is not transmitted by the application.

Cookies

The application itself does not set, read or use cookies.

Analytics & tracking

The application does not intentionally use analytics or tracking. There are no analytics scripts, telemetry, identifiers or third-party cookies in the shipped build.

Third-party services

MeroPrayas does not depend on any remote services to work. All assets — the Poppins, Preeti and Hisab fonts, icons, and the processing libraries used by the PDF tools (pdf-lib, pdf.js and JSZip) — are self-hosted or vendored with the application. No external CDNs, remote fonts, embedded services or analytics are required.

Like any web page, MeroPrayas must reach your browser's own device, but after the app has loaded you can continue using it without a network connection.

Children's privacy

MeroPrayas is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. Because the application does not collect personal information, no such information is knowingly collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. When it is updated, the date at the top of this page will be revised. Please review it occasionally.

Frequently asked questions

Is MeroPrayas really private and offline?

Yes. Every tool runs in your browser and files are never uploaded to a server. After your first visit the app works offline too.

Does the app collect any data about me?

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking scripts. Only your theme and last image settings are stored locally on your own device.

Why did my converted Nepali text come out wrong?

Make sure the "From" format matches the text you pasted — Preeti text in the Unicode field won't convert correctly. Check both selects and try the swap (⇄) button.

What image formats does the compressor support?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and SVG are accepted, and you can export to JPG, PNG or WebP at your chosen size and quality.

How fast will I get a reply to my email?

Usually within a few days. For quicker questions, the WhatsApp channel is the fastest way to reach me.
